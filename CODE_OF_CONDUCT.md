# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want WealthBrain to be a technical project where people can discuss architecture, research, experiments, and implementation without harassment or discrimination.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- focus criticism on ideas and code;
- acknowledge evidence and uncertainty;
- avoid personal attacks;
- respect project boundaries and private data.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may result in moderation, removal of contributions, or exclusion from project spaces at the maintainer's discretion.
